Our Story

Why we started

Kulver & Preeti began with a simple belief: a photograph and a website should feel just as considered as the moment or brand behind them.

What started as retouching photographs for photographers and families grew into a full creative practice. Over the years we added website development — because clients kept asking for a digital home as polished as their images. Today we do both, side by side, so your visuals and your website speak the same premium language.

Every project, whether a single portrait or a complete Shopify store, gets the same careful attention: thoughtful direction, meticulous execution, and honest communication from brief to delivery.
